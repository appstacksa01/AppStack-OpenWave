These grants apply only to rights the contributors can license. Linked papers, manuals, frequency lists, recordings, screenshots, supplier material, and separately marked third-party code retain their own rights. A citation or download link does not grant redistribution rights. Do not import third-party material without documenting permission or a compatible licence.

Logos, branding assets and trademarks are outside these default grants unless explicitly licensed separately. These licences do not grant endorsement or trademark rights. Hardware designs are not licensed by this policy; choose and publish appropriate terms if designs are later contributed.

The licence texts themselves retain their publishers' notices. This policy describes their application to the project; it does not modify their terms or add restrictions to licensed software or documentation.

## Contributions and commercial activity

By submitting an original contribution for inclusion, contributors agree to license it under the applicable terms above and confirm they have the necessary rights. Disclose third-party material and its terms in the pull request. Submit any proposed exception for review before inclusion; do not assume that a different licence will be accepted.

Commercial activity, sponsorship and paid development are compatible with these licences. GPL obligations do not require an upstream pull request or a donation. Follow the full licence when conveying covered software. Original backend code is covered by the software policy above; adopting server processing did not change the project to AGPLv3. Any future licence change requires separate review.
