# Software and supplier research

Reviewed: 22 October 2026. Supplier pages document their stated functionality. They do not independently establish performance or medical effectiveness.

## RifeMedic SA

The [Documents page](https://www.rifemedicsa.co.za/Documents/) lists model-specific program lists, manuals, upgrades, and RifeMedic 5 PC software described as free to use [S13]. The software and selected linked PDFs could not be retrieved during this review; their contents remain unverified.

The [About page](https://rifemedicsa.co.za/About/) describes a Windows frequency generator with two channels, oscilloscope displays, and spectrum analysers [S14]. WAV export, file specifications, measurement capabilities, compatibility with the Electro-Med 235p, and any relationship to the missing original app have not been established.

Availability at no charge does not establish permission to reuse source code or redistribute bundled lists. Obtain and review the actual license before reuse. No installer was run and no supplier was contacted.

## Cell Nutrition: FIT Rife I

The [product description](https://www.cellnutrition.co.za/product/fit-rife-i-handheld-electrode-frequency-device) describes a battery-powered handheld-electrode device using voltage pulses. It distinguishes this from a FIT II designed for a coil [S15]. The inspected page lacks sufficient numerical pulse/output and isolation specifications for replication.

This is a separate device family, not an identified replacement or equivalent for the Electro-Med 235p. No performance measurements or compatibility tests have been performed.

## Online frequency lists

The owner recalls that the missing app accepted frequency values obtained online and generated an audio file. Treat this as an owner account, not verified behaviour of the RifeMedic software.

Before importing a list, establish:

- Source, author, version, and reuse rights.
- Whether numbers represent hertz, a carrier, modulation rate, or instrument-specific settings.
- Waveform, duty cycle, dwell time, amplitude representation, and channel mapping.
- Which descriptions are source claims and what evidence supports them.

A frequency alone does not specify an electrical signal. An audio waveform, electrode pulse, and radiated RF field are different output types.
