# Research entry template

## Question and investigation ID

State the answerable question and relevant INV item.

## Source or measurement

- Author/organization and title:
- Publication date/version:
- Stable URL/DOI and source ID:
- Access/review date:
- Exact inspected pages/sections:
- Type: historical claim / patent / manual / platform docs / study / project measurement.
- Status: located / partial review / reviewed stated portions.

Measurements also need device identity, instruments/calibration, diagram, test load, settings, method, raw data, uncertainty, and bench safety review. No person is part of the initial test circuit.

## Claim or observation

Separate the source author's interpretation from the observation. Paraphrase briefly.

## Assessment

What supports or contradicts this? What cannot this source establish? Can another contributor independently check or reproduce it?

## Project implication

Does a requirement change, or does a question remain open? Do not infer medical effectiveness from electrical measurements.

## Review and next action

Reviewer/date, unresolved questions, follow-up investigation.
